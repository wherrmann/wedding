Where to stay

Lodging

The Catskills are a popular vacation and wedding destination in the summer, so we recommend booking 4-6 months in advance.

We have reserved the Gateway Lodge at Pine Hill for our guests. This is a relatively small property with only 11 rooms, so we anticipate it booking up quickly.

How to Book Use the link below to book. You can also call the lodge to book and mention the Herrmann-Kerson wedding.

Other Nearby Options

There are plenty of other great options nearby. We recommend looking into:

Margaretville Mountain Inn

A quaint bed and breakfast with affordable rates, planned shuttle stop (see below)

Catskills Season Lodge

A nearby inn with a pool!

Shandaken Inn

Beautiful upscale inn with a pool and tennis courts!

Breezy Hill Inn

Where we stayed on our venue hunt trip - delicious breakfast and helpful hosts! It is up a small hill so will likely not be a shuttle stop.

The Roxbury Experience

Very eclectic “experience” hotel with themed rooms, you have been warned! Also the closest accommodations to the wedding venue and a planned shuttle stop.

Rentals

Airbnb and VRBO rentals are widely available in the Roxbury area.
